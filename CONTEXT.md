# Keepsake

Keepsake archives content from Instagram profiles. Its two browsing modes are
Highlights and Posts.

## Language

**Highlight**: A named, ordered collection of saved Instagram stories on a
profile. One highlight can contain many stories.

**Story**: An individual photo or video published as an Instagram story and
included in a highlight. Story counts describe the contents of highlights.

**Post**: An entry in a profile's Instagram feed, including a photo, video, reel,
or carousel. A carousel is one post regardless of how many slides it contains.

**Post media file**: An individual photo or video belonging to a post. A
three-slide carousel has one post and three media files.

**Saved library**: Downloaded content belonging to a profile, browsed as either
saved highlights and their stories or saved posts and their media files.
